How a project runs

Clear steps, written agreements and working software you can see early.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We learn your goals, users and constraints, then agree scope, timeline and cost in writing.

  2. 2

    Design

    Wireframes, architecture and a clickable prototype, so you see the product before we build it.

  3. 3

    Build

    Short sprints, each ending with a working demo. You see real progress every one to two weeks.

  4. 4

    Launch and support

    We deploy, monitor and hand over documentation, then stay on for fixes and new features.
